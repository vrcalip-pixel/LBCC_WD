★ Flagship offering

AI Upskilling for Working Teams

Practical, no-hype sessions that get your staff using AI safely and well — built from LBCC's A.S. in AI for Digital Transformation, the first dedicated AI degree in the California Community College system, and rebuilt as short modules for working professionals.

01

AI Foundations for the Workplace

What generative AI is, what it isn't, and how to put it to work without putting your data at risk.

2 hours

Half day

Evaluating AI Output

2 hours
04

AI for Business Communication

Half day
05

AI for Data & Decisions

AI Ethics, Policy & Governance

Custom
